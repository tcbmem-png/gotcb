01 / The diagnosis

The pattern is the same in every shop.

01

Too many tools

POS, scheduling, invoicing, messaging, marketing — all paid for, none of them talking to each other.

02

No single source of truth

If three people had to find a number, you'd get three different answers — pulled from three different places.

03

The owner is the system

Everything routes through you. Step away for a weekend and the wheels start coming off.

04

Memory and group texts

The most important records in your business live in someone's head and a 200-message thread.

You're not missing effort. You're missing structure.

02 / The shift

Rails first. Intelligence second.

Most owners try to bolt automation onto a business that doesn't have a system underneath it yet. That's why it breaks. You can't automate guesswork.

The order matters. Build the rails — the path every lead, every job, every dollar takes through your business. Make the rules explicit. Get visibility on what's actually happening. Then layer in automation where it pays.

03 / The system

Six modules. One operating system.

01

Leads & Intake

Every inquiry — call, text, form, walk-in — captured in one place, qualified the same way every time.

02

Jobs, Events & Projects

The work itself — quoted, scheduled, assigned, executed, closed. Same flow whether it's a haircut or a wedding.

03

Payments & Deposits

Money collected at the right moment in the workflow — deposits at booking, balances at close, all reconciled.

04

Communication Hub

Customer threads, crew chatter, and confirmations — unified, threaded to the job, not lost in personal phones.

05

Tasks & Staff

Who's doing what, by when. Recurring routines, daily checklists, and accountability without micromanagement.

06

Daily Dashboard

One screen that tells you whether today is going well — leads, jobs, money, exceptions. The whole business at a glance.
